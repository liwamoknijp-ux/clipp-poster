CLIPP Poster

A small personal publishing tool for the TikTok account @chatlostit7x7 (CLIPP).

CLIPP makes short vertical gaming clips from livestreams, inside official streamer clipping campaigns (for example the LACY GTA VI Marathon campaign on Whop). CLIPP Poster lets the account owner publish those finished clips to their own TikTok account at planned times, from their own computer, instead of uploading each video by hand.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and allows CLIPP Poster to publish videos to their account.
  2. The owner picks a finished clip, its caption and hashtags, and its privacy setting.
  3. CLIPP Poster uploads the video file with the TikTok Content Posting API and reports the publish status.

CLIPP Poster is used only by the account owner. It does not read, collect or store any other TikTok users' data, does not post comments or messages, and never posts without the owner choosing the video and caption.
