Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

CLIPP Poster ("the app") is a personal publishing tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @chatlostit7x7 (CLIPP). This policy explains what the app does with data.

Data the app uses

  • TikTok account information from TikTok Login Kit (the user.info.basic scope): open ID, display name and avatar of the signed-in account, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to publish videos the owner chooses.
  • Videos and captions the owner chooses to publish.

How data is stored

Tokens and videos stay on the owner's own computer. The app has no server database and does not send data to any third party other than TikTok's own API.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), which revokes the tokens, and can delete the local token file. Questions: send a message to @chatlostit7x7 on TikTok.
